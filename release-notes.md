# StreamHub iOS 1.3.0

## New: YouTube
- A **YouTube** tab: sign in on Google's own page (StreamHub never sees your password). Your sign-in is kept when you install new versions, like Twitch and Kick.
- **Home** (your recommendations) and **Subscriptions** (new videos grouped by day: Today, Yesterday, weekdays…), loading more as you scroll; pull down to refresh.
- **Search** videos and channels, and channel pages.
- **No ads:** videos play in YouTube's own player with the ads taken out before it sees them and ad servers blocked. If YouTube still starts one, StreamHub covers it while it's muted and skipped.
- **Plays in place:** tap a video and it plays right there in the list; the expand button opens a bigger player (⌄ goes back to the list, still playing; × stops). Videos start muted: tap the player to unmute.

## SponsorBlock
- Sponsors, self-promotion and "like and subscribe" reminders are skipped automatically.
- **Settings → YouTube · SponsorBlock:** for each category (sponsor, self-promotion, interaction reminder, intro, outro, preview/recap, filler, non-music part) choose Skip automatically, Show a Skip button, or Don't skip.

## Test builds
- Test builds are also a direct IPA download (not a zip), always at the same link, for signing services that take a link. The update banner ignores them.

## Install
If you have 1.2.0, the update banner offers this version: **Get it** can copy the IPA's link or download the IPA for your signing service. Or download **StreamHub-iOS-1.3.0-unsigned.ipa** below. Use the same bundle ID as before so it replaces the app and keeps your settings and sign-ins. The `.sha256` file is its checksum.

The ad-free Twitch servers are run by the TTV LOL PRO / Luminous community and see which channel you open and your IP address.
