# StreamHub iOS 1.5.0

## YouTube comments
- A video's comments show under the bigger player (the speech-bubble button under a playing video opens it), loading more as you scroll.
- **Reply** to a comment, or **Add a comment**: it's posted publicly as your signed-in YouTube account and shows straight away.
- Settings → YouTube → **Show comments** turns them off.

## Watch later
- **Watch later** on the row under Home | Subscriptions | History: your Watch later list, with ⋯ → Remove from Watch later.
- ⋯ → **Save to Watch later** on any video.

## Filter by channel
- **Filter by channel** lists every channel you're subscribed to; picking one shows its latest videos. ✕ goes back.

## Install
The update banner offers this version: **Get it** can copy the IPA's link or download the IPA for your signing service. Or download **StreamHub-iOS-1.5.0-unsigned.ipa** below. Use the same bundle ID as before so it replaces the app and keeps your settings and sign-ins. The `.sha256` file is its checksum.

The ad-free Twitch servers are run by the TTV LOL PRO / Luminous community and see which channel you open and your IP address.
