# StreamHub iOS 1.7.0

## YouTube Shorts
- A **Shorts** tab next to Home (Home · Shorts · Subs · History): one Short at a time, filling the screen and looping. Swipe up for the next, down for the one before; tap to pause.
- No ads, and SponsorBlock as usual. In the best quality YouTube has.
- Starts muted: the sound button turns it on, and it stays on for the next Shorts. Share sends the Short's link.
- The next Shorts load in the background while you watch, so swiping starts the next one straight away.

## Also
- The Settings cog sits on the same line as StreamHub.

## Install
The update banner offers this version: **Get it** can copy the IPA's link or download the IPA for your signing service. Or download **StreamHub-iOS-1.7.0-unsigned.ipa** below. Use the same bundle ID as before so it replaces the app and keeps your settings and sign-ins. The `.sha256` file is its checksum.

The ad-free Twitch servers are run by the TTV LOL PRO / Luminous community and see which channel you open and your IP address.
