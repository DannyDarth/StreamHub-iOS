# StreamHub iOS 1.4.0

## YouTube History
- A **History** tab next to Home and Subscriptions: your YouTube watch history, grouped by day, loading more as you scroll.
- **⋯ → Remove from watch history** takes a video out of your YouTube history (YouTube's own removal).
- Videos you watch in StreamHub show up there too, and pick up where you left off, because they play in YouTube's own player.

## Faster SponsorBlock
- Segments start loading the moment you open a video, use SponsorBlock's faster lookup, and are remembered for 6 hours.

## Full screen
- The full-screen button under a playing video now goes straight to iPhone's full-screen player, like YouTube's own button.

## Install
If you have 1.3.0, the update banner offers this version: **Get it** can copy the IPA's link or download the IPA for your signing service. Or download **StreamHub-iOS-1.4.0-unsigned.ipa** below. Use the same bundle ID as before so it replaces the app and keeps your settings and sign-ins. The `.sha256` file is its checksum.

The ad-free Twitch servers are run by the TTV LOL PRO / Luminous community and see which channel you open and your IP address.
