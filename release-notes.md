# StreamHub iOS 1.2.0

## New: Crunchyroll
- A **Crunchyroll** tab next to Twitch and Kick, with crunchyroll.com itself: sign in on Crunchyroll's own page (StreamHub never sees your password), browse, search, use your watchlist and watch in Crunchyroll's player.
- Pages fit the phone's screen, and the player's **Full screen** button plays the video in iPhone's own full-screen player.
- Back, forward, reload, home and **Open in Safari** at the top; swipe from the edge to go back. Switching to Twitch or Kick and back keeps your place.
- On an episode page, **App** opens the episode in the Crunchyroll app, if you'd rather watch there.
- Episodes are copy-protected, so they play only in Crunchyroll's own player: nothing is downloaded, recorded or bypassed.

## Sign-ins survive new versions
- Your Twitch, Kick and Crunchyroll sign-ins are kept when you install a new version, so no more signing in and two-factor after every install. They're saved in the iPhone's Keychain and never leave the device.
- Settings → Sign-ins → **Sign out of Twitch, Kick and Crunchyroll** signs out and forgets the saved copy.

## Update check
- A banner tells you when a new StreamHub iOS version is out. **Get it** can copy the IPA's link or download the IPA for your signing service (Signulous, AltStore, Sideloadly…), or open the release page.
- Settings → Updates: turn it off, **Check now**, and the installed version.

## New icon
- The new StreamHub icon, the same as StreamHub desktop 2.2.0.

## Install
**StreamHub-iOS-1.2.0-unsigned.ipa** isn't signed: sign and install it with your signing service, using the same bundle ID as before so it replaces the app and keeps your settings. The `.sha256` file is its checksum.

The ad-free Twitch servers are run by the TTV LOL PRO / Luminous community and see which channel you open and your IP address.
