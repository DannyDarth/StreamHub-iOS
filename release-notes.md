# StreamHub iOS 1.6.0

## Select services to activate
- Settings → **Select services to activate**: a switch for Twitch, Kick, YouTube and Crunchyroll.
- A service that's off is hidden from the main screen's tabs, Followed and its Settings sections until you turn it back on. Its sign-in and settings are kept. The last service on can't be turned off.

## YouTube ⋯ menu
- **Share** a video's YouTube link with iPhone's share sheet.
- **Not interested** and **Don't recommend channel** on recommended videos (Home): YouTube's own feedback, and the video leaves the list.

## Install
The update banner offers this version: **Get it** can copy the IPA's link or download the IPA for your signing service. Or download **StreamHub-iOS-1.6.0-unsigned.ipa** below. Use the same bundle ID as before so it replaces the app and keeps your settings and sign-ins. The `.sha256` file is its checksum.

The ad-free Twitch servers are run by the TTV LOL PRO / Luminous community and see which channel you open and your IP address.
